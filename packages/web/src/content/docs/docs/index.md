title: Welcome to Kowork
description: Learn how to set up Kowork and complete work with AI in a folder on your computer.
---

Kowork helps you complete work with AI using everyday language. Choose a folder, describe the result you want, and Kowork can read, create, and organize files there for you to review.

## Start here

1. [Install Kowork](/docs/getting-started/install/) on your computer.
2. [Connect an AI Provider](/docs/getting-started/connect-provider/) so Kowork can do the work.
3. [Create Your First Task](/docs/getting-started/first-task/) by choosing a folder and describing the outcome you need.

## Learn the essentials

- [Folders and Files](/docs/using-kowork/folders-and-files/) explains how Kowork works with your files.
- [Tasks and Subtasks](/docs/using-kowork/tasks-and-subtasks/) covers starting work and breaking larger jobs into smaller parts.
- [Create Office Documents](/docs/using-kowork/office-documents/) shows how to make documents, spreadsheets, presentations, and PDFs.
- [Permissions and Safety](/docs/using-kowork/permissions/) explains how to review and control requested actions.

## Make Kowork yours

Choose your preferred AI in [Providers and Models](/docs/customize/providers-and-models/), connect other tools and information with [Connectors](/docs/customize/connectors/), or add abilities with [Skills](/docs/customize/skills/). You can also adjust Kowork in [Settings](/docs/customize/settings/).

If something is not working, visit [Troubleshooting](/docs/help/troubleshooting/).
